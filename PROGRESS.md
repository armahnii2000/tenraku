## Background settled this session
- Toured Tatham's Portable Puzzle Collection (exactly 40 generator types) puzzle by puzzle; Nikoli = reader-submitted, hand-made, credited authors (~300 types); Tatham = generated, unique-solution guaranteed.
- IP picture (general info, not legal advice): puzzle *rules/mechanics* are free to use commercially; Nikoli's actual published *grids* are not; *names* carry trademark risk mainly in Japan ("Sudoku" is Nikoli's registered mark there). Falling's pattern — borrow rules, transform them, own levels/art/name — is the standard safe one.
- Japanese title materials noted: 転落 *tenraku* (fall from grace), 闇 *yami* (darkness), 謎 *nazo* (riddle) vs パズル *pazuru* (loanword).
