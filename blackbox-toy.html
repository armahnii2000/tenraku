Black Box — learn it by firing

3 balls are hidden in the box. Light travels straight.
A ball dead ahead stops it. A ball at its corner pushes it 90° away.
Tap any yellow rim square to fire a ray in.

When you think you know where the balls are, switch to Mark balls, tap 3 cells, and check.

Fire a few rays. Watch where they come out — the mismatch is the clue.
