Dominosa — the small version

Only 10 dominoes (numbers 0–3). Cover the board by pairing neighbouring numbers.
Every pair exactly once: 0-0, 0-1, 0-2, 0-3, 1-1, 1-2, 1-3, 2-2, 2-3, 3-3.
Tap one number, then tap a neighbour to join them into a domino. Tap a green domino to remove it.

Start by highlighting a number above and hunting for a pair that appears only once next to each other — that domino is forced.
