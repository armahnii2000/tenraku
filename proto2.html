CMY Light Puzzles — prototype v0.2 · prisms

Drag dot to dot. Filters remove colours. A PRISM splits your light into one beam per colour still in it — a coloured prism eats its opposite colour first, so it splits into 2 beams instead of 3. Every beam must reach the COLLECTOR black: find the one filter that kills each beam's colour. Beams may cross each other's trails.

Drag from START to begin.
